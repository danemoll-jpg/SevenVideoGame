**MARS — THE DIVIDED KINGDOMS**

**Chapter structure**

Arrival → disputed crossing → encounter with Viper → investigate both sides → restore access to the temple → subdue Viper → historical revelation → receive record and new coordinates → saucer conversation.

*All visuals remain grayscale. Panther speaks telepathically. Use laughs, breaths, and pauses naturally. Facial animation is optional; posture and camera framing carry the performances.*

---

**1. ARRIVAL — LINES ACROSS A WORLD**

**EXTREME WIDE — MARS**

The saucer crosses an immense plain cut by walls, trenches, and elevated roads.

Two inhabited fortress cities stand on opposite sides of a deep ravine. Between them, an ancient temple rises from a central island of stone.

Its architecture predates the fortifications surrounding it.

**INT. SAUCER — PILOT’S CHAIR**

Panther rests in 7’s lap.

**7:** There’s the temple.

**PANTHER:** And several people determined to make reaching it inconvenient.

She studies the bridges. Both approaches are barricaded.

**7 — a small sigh:** The guardian did mention borders.

**PANTHER:** Mm. They appear to have been thorough.

**GAMEPLAY**

Land outside the occupied approaches. The bike lowers out of the bike hold; 7 descends through the saucer’s underside beam (saucer rule, Dan 2026-11-19: the bike always uses the bike hold, the beam is only for 7 and Panther).

Panther stays aboard.

**OBJECTIVE:** Find a route to the temple.

---

**2. THE CROSSING — A SMALL PROBLEM WITH REAL PEOPLE**

7 rides through a settlement toward the nearest bridge.

The inhabitants are Reptilians: workers, families, traders, and guards. Establish ordinary life before introducing the conflict.

At a checkpoint, a supply transport is stranded behind a closed barrier.

**MEDIUM — 7 AND A GUARD**

**7:** I’m looking for the temple.

**GUARD:** Crossing is closed.

**7:** For how long?

**GUARD:** Until the other side withdraws.

Across the checkpoint, a worker gestures toward the supplies.

**WORKER:** Those are water filters. They’re waiting for them.

**GUARD:** I have my orders.

7 looks from the supplies to the bridge.

**7:** Who gives them?

A low mechanical sound interrupts.

---

**3. VIPER — SOMEONE PROFITING FROM THE DIVISION**

**LOW-ANGLE REVEAL**

Viper arrives above the checkpoint on an elevated platform. Harsh white light from beneath emphasizes his silhouette.

*His exact appearance and equipment should follow Kyle’s design when supplied.*

**VIPER:** Someone who understands what happens when we become careless.

**7:** They need those supplies.

**VIPER:** Then they can accept our terms.

**7:** And the temple?

Viper notices the geometric objects she carries.

**VIPER:** What business do you have there?

She shifts slightly, keeping them close.

**7:** Historical.

**VIPER — a dry, dismissive laugh:** History is precisely the problem.

He orders the crossing secured and leaves.

**PANTHER — privately:** Charming.

**7:** He noticed the records.

**PANTHER:** I noticed him noticing.

**OBJECTIVE:** Find another way across.

---

**4. BOTH SIDES OF THE RAVINE**

**GAMEPLAY — HOVER BIKE AND EXPLORATION**

The player follows an old maintenance route beneath the main crossing.

Use broken causeways, sheltered passages, and broad riding sections. Include clear stopping points for exploration.

7 helps reopen the supply route without attacking the checkpoint guards. The transport can cross through a restored service passage.

On the opposite side, she meets a Reptilian caretaker responsible for maintaining the temple approach.

**CARETAKER:** You brought the filters through?

**7:** Helped them through.

**CARETAKER:** Hm. We were told nobody over there would let them leave.

**7:** The people carrying them seemed quite determined.

The caretaker looks toward the opposite settlement.

**CARETAKER:** That is not what we’ve been hearing.

**STORY PURPOSE**

Show that ordinary people on both sides are capable of cooperation. Their distrust has sources; do not portray them as foolish or inherently warlike.

---

**5. THE SHARED DOOR**

The caretaker takes 7 to an ancient mechanism overlooking the temple.

Two channels lead into it from opposite sides of the ravine.

**OVER 7’S SHOULDER — MECHANISM**

**7:** Two controls?

**CARETAKER:** One on each bank.

**7:** So both sides have to open it.

**CARETAKER:** That was the intention.

**PANTHER:** Rather optimistic architecture.

**7 — quietly:** Or they used to trust each other.

The caretaker explains that Viper has disabled the shared approach and uses its closure to control passage between the settlements.

**CARETAKER:** He tells each bank the other intends to seize the temple.

**7:** Does either one?

**CARETAKER:** Some would. Others just want the bridge open.

**OBJECTIVE:** Restore both temple controls.

---

**6. TWO CONTROLS — A PLAYABLE COOPERATION SEQUENCE**

7 returns to the first settlement with evidence that the service route is safe and the caretaker is willing to help.

The worker from the supply transport volunteers to operate one control. The caretaker operates the other.

7 must restore the connections between them.

**GAMEPLAY**

Combine:

- Riding between separated access points.
- On-foot traversal through ancient bridge structures.
- Redirecting power or repositioning mechanisms using existing abilities.
- Protecting the repair effort through nonlethal interventions.

Panther helps her coordinate from the saucer.

**PANTHER:** The far control is ready.

**7:** Tell them to hold.

**PANTHER:** They are holding.

A mechanism moves unexpectedly.

**7 — a startled breath:** Not that one!

**PANTHER:** Specificity. Always helpful.

She secures it.

**7 — a short, relieved laugh:** Now.

The two inhabitants activate their controls together.

**WIDE — THE BRIDGE**

Ancient structures align, forming a passage toward the temple.

---

**7. VIPER’S INTERVENTION**

Viper moves to seize the restored crossing before others can use it.

**WIDE — TEMPLE APPROACH**

He blocks 7’s path.

**VIPER:** Do you know what you’ve opened?

**7:** A bridge.

**VIPER:** A way for them to come through.

**7:** It goes both ways.

**VIPER:** That is what concerns me.

He activates the crossing’s defensive machinery.

**PROPOSED ENCOUNTER**

Viper uses barriers and moving sections to divide the arena. The player reconnects routes, disables his control points, and closes the distance.

Avoid copying Venus’s creature-control fight. This encounter is about positioning, timing, and denying Viper control of the space.

7 subdues and contains him without killing him.

**LEVEL TWO-SHOT — AFTER THE FIGHT**

**VIPER:** They’ll turn on each other again.

7 looks toward the worker and caretaker approaching from opposite sides.

**7:** That’s their choice. You don’t get to make it for them.

Leave Viper secured for local custody.

This does not end every conflict on Mars. It removes one person’s immediate control over this crossing and temple.

---

**8. THE TEMPLE — HISTORY THAT BELONGS TO BOTH**

The worker and caretaker enter with 7.

Inside, the temple’s markings incorporate symbols now used separately by the two kingdoms.

**INSERT — THE SYMBOLS TOGETHER**

**WORKER:** That’s ours.

**CARETAKER:** And that is ours.

**7:** They’re part of the same design.

Hold on the complete pattern.

They approach Mars’s geometric rune.

**DESIGN NOTE (Dan, 2026-11-19):** Mars’s rune is a **tetrahedron**, generated in code like the cube, dodecahedron and icosahedron.

---

**9. MARS’S VISION — PROPOSED**

**VISION VIDEO (Dan, 2026-11-19):** `Art/Video/vision_04_mars.mp4` (12 s, 1920×1044; still fallback `Resources/Mars/vision_04_still.jpg`). It shows the bridge being completed in the past: the great arch over the ravine with its cradle, two Reptilians on opposite banks guiding the centre span down into place between them until it joins both sides, the white cities in the mist behind, a planet in the sky. Its own audio is nearly silent — add soft stone-on-stone and wind, no music (as the other visions). The carved panel and the vague figure aren’t in the video; the joining span carries the same meaning, and 7’s lines (“They built it together.” / “The bridge. Both sides.”) fit it as written.

7 touches the rune.

**VISION — ENTIRELY IN THE PAST**

The same bridge is intact, without checkpoints or fortifications.

Two groups approach from opposite banks, carrying sections of a large carved panel. Their clothing differs, but they work together.

They lift the sections into place. The separate designs form the complete symbol seen inside the temple.

An indistinct figure pauses beside the work and offers a familiar gesture of acknowledgment toward someone just outside the frame.

No spoken dialogue. Faces remain vague. The viewpoint does not interact.

**RETURN — INSERT OF 7’S HAND LEAVING THE RUNE**

**7:** They built it together.

**CARETAKER:** The temple?

**7:** The bridge. Both sides.

The worker looks toward the entrance.

**WORKER:** We were taught it was taken from us.

**CARETAKER:** So were we.

Let the silence carry the discovery.

**7 — softly:** Then people should see this.

They agree to preserve and share the record. Under the proposed collection system, 7 receives a small counterpart while the original remains.

---

**10. TWO NEW LEADS**

As the temple systems reactivate, a display shows its old connections.

**OVER 7’S SHOULDER — TWO LOCATIONS**

One lies among asteroids. The other is Biaveh.

**CARETAKER:** These were places our keepers exchanged records with.

**7:** The Elarim gave us locations too.

**CARETAKER:** Then perhaps you can reach them.

**WORKER:** What do you expect to find?

7 looks at the record in her hands.

**7:** I’m trying not to decide before I get there.

She sends the locations to the saucer.

**PANTHER:** Received. An asteroid world and Biaveh.

**7:** We’ll look at them together.

**PROGRESSION**

Unlock the asteroid world and Biaveh.

Europa remains available if unfinished. Do not mark a completed Europa mission as a new lead.

---

**11. RETURN — THE SMALL CHANGE THAT MATTERS**

On the ride back, the supply transport crosses openly.

The guard and caretaker exchange a few practical words. They are not suddenly close friends, but they are working.

7 slows to let the transport pass.

**PANTHER:** Did you settle their differences?

**7:** No.

She watches the crossing.

**7:** Got them talking.

**PANTHER:** A productive afternoon, then.

She gives a quiet chuckle and continues toward the saucer.

---

**12. ABOARD THE SAUCER — WHAT SHE REMEMBERS**

7 sits in the pilot’s chair. Panther climbs into her lap.

**7:** They were both so certain.

**PANTHER:** About the bridge?

She nods, stroking him.

**7:** What if I’m doing that? Taking little pieces and deciding what they mean?

**PANTHER — gently:** Then keep asking.

**7:** And if I get it wrong?

He looks up at her.

**PANTHER:** I’ll mention it.

She laughs.

**7:** Oh, I know you will.

He settles as she brings up the navigation display.

---

**13. DESTINATION CHOICE**

**IF EUROPA IS UNFINISHED**

**PANTHER:** Three places to look. Europa, the asteroid world, and Biaveh.

**7:** What do the new records tell us?

**PANTHER:** A machine-age temple among the asteroids. Another on Biaveh, among the Biaviian.

**7:** And the Cetaceans beneath Europa’s ice.

**PANTHER:** Mm-hmm. That one remains underwater.

**7 — amused:** You haven’t forgotten.

**PANTHER:** Remarkable powers of recollection.

**PLAYER CHOICES**

- **Europa:** Seek the Cetacean temple beneath the ice.
- **Asteroid world:** Find the Great Machine Age temple.
- **Biaveh:** Find the temple of the Connected Mind Age.

**IF EUROPA IS COMPLETE**

**PANTHER:** Two new leads. The asteroid world and Biaveh.

**7:** Machinery or minds.

**PANTHER:** I suspect both will be more complicated than that.

**7 — a small laugh:** They usually are.

**PLAYER CHOICES**

- **Asteroid world:** Find the Great Machine Age temple.
- **Biaveh:** Find the temple of the Connected Mind Age.

**IN BOTH VERSIONS**

**7 — looking down at Panther:** Comfortable?

He shifts slightly in her lap.

**PANTHER:** You may proceed.

She smiles in her voice as she selects the course.

**END OF MARS CHAPTER**
