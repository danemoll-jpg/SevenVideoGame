> **Status (2026-11-20):** chapter script from Dan. Not scheduled yet (next chapter to build). Open decisions are listed below
> the script under "Notes from the chat".

## Chapter premise

The asteroid contains the remains of a Great Machine Age civilization: foundries, transport tunnels, enormous moving assemblies and a temple incorporated into the city’s central engine.

Its caretaker, the Machinist, continues his original duty: keep the city ready for its inhabitants’ return. With supplies exhausted, he has begun recycling unused structures—including the archive.

7 must stop the destruction without shutting down the systems that keep the asteroid stable.

Panther remains aboard the saucer for most of the mission, communicating telepathically. Their growing familiarity gives the chapter its warmth: teasing during exploration, genuine concern during danger, and quiet companionship afterward.

**Chapter reward:** the machine civilization’s preserved history and its rune/key. Its geometric shape remains to be selected.

**Progression:** both this chapter and Biaveh must be completed before the Cattalus lead resolves.

### Main gameplay sequence

Reach three controls in a compact, interconnected factory area:

1. **Transport control:** reposition cargo platforms to cross a gap.
2. **Cooling control:** redirect coolant to open a safe passage through the foundry.
3. **Archive diverter:** move intact archive panels onto a storage loop.

Each completed control visibly changes the central hall. The player repeatedly sees the archive getting closer to safety.

Security machines try to escort or restrain 7. She evades them, traps them behind barriers or temporarily disables them. No killing.

### Nonlethal boss encounter

The Machinist tries to restrain 7 and restore the recycling line.

The player must lure his heavy maintenance tools into three service stations, then activate their clamps. Each station locks one tool assembly and opens the next route.

- Clear mechanical wind-ups make attacks readable.
- Moving cover and conveyor lanes create positional challenges.
- Panther calls out useful observations, with hints escalating only if needed.
- Final action: engage the maintenance cradle, immobilizing the Machinist without destroying him.

## Implementation notes

- Required order: **Mars + Europa → departure for Biaveh → asteroid discovery → Biaveh and asteroid in either order → Cattalus → final Moon.**
- The asteroid cannot be selected before its travel discovery.
- The chapter contains one main nonlethal encounter, three linked machinery puzzles and bike traversal.
- Optional inspections deepen the history but never block progression.
- Panther’s concern during the lost connection and their reunion are mandatory relationship scenes.
- Dialogue pauses, chuckles and breaths should sound incidental. Avoid placing a vocal mannerism on every line.
- The caretaker’s exact visual design, the rune’s shape and the shared Cattalus clue need approval before becoming fixed lore.

## Decisions (Dan, 2026-11-20)

- **The Machinist's look:** Kyle's "Terminator-type" sketch (`Art/Reference/kyle_terminator_villain.jpg`), **adapted**: no
  cape; the arm cannon becomes a **heavy maintenance tool arm** (cutter / clamp). Riveted steam-age machine, narrow dome
  helmet with a slit visor, flared collar with two exhaust stacks, glowing round chest core, belt of gauges and canisters,
  the front tabard as a stiff riveted plate with glyphs and the crescent emblem, piston-jointed legs.
- **Rune: cuboctahedron** (squares and triangles around one centre — "vector equilibrium"), generated in code like the others.
- **AST_10 reunion: keep it, but no gap / lock-up** — cut straight from the beam-up into it (no walking, no waiting), a few
  seconds, then into the chair scene.
- **The fight + a new weapon (Dan: "earlier we discussed an energy weapon … kind of like a light saber but not quite"):**
  7 gets **arc blades** here — chat's proposal, Dan to confirm the details:
  - a pair of **curved, crescent-shaped blades of white light** (Kyle's sketch `kyle_7_blades_sketch.jpg`: 7 with two long
    curved blades crossed behind her), flickering / ribbon-like, a soft hum — not straight lightsabers;
  - the Machinists' **repair tools for cutting and re-joining energy lines**: against machines they sever power and short
    out systems; against living beings they only **stun** (7 never kills);
  - found in **AST_05**: a sealed maintenance locker by the terminal; she handles them as if familiar. Proposed lines:
    `AST_05_10` Panther — "You've done that before." / `AST_05_11` 7 — "I… don't know." (looking at her hands);
  - **the Machinist fight** follows the "Boss fights" rule: telekinesis to stun him (throw scrap / cargo) → close in →
    the arc blades on his exposed **power couplings** (disabling, not destroying) → his energy bar drains; he recovers and
    attacks (tool-arm swings, clamps, conveyor lanes, his restraint attempts); the three service-station clamps can lock a
    tool arm at the end of each phase; the maintenance cradle immobilizes him at the end (AST_07_14 / 15). Panther's
    "enormous arm" lines stay;
  - she keeps the blades for later chapters (her kit grows).
