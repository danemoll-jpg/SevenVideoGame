# 7 The Observer

## The opening

7 is imprisoned on Earth’s hollow Moon, where Tunneler controls a mining operation and exploits an alien people called the Scree. The reason for her imprisonment is initially a mystery.

Her cell's laser field dampens her powers — her energy point stays dark until she is free. A black cat arrives to rescue her. She assumes he is an ordinary animal until he communicates telepathically. The feline race sent him, but he does not know the larger purpose behind his assignment.

They meet for the first time during the rescue. He helps her escape, and she comes to trust him through his actions. Their first main objective is to recover her confiscated saucer.

The proposed escape takes them through ancient structures beneath the mining facility. Touching a ruin triggers a historical vision. Its exact contents remain undecided, but the intended function is to help her progress while revealing something she cannot explain. They then emerge from the confined passages into an open area, and 7 looks back: for the first time she sees the city in its full majesty — the prison is only a small part of it, and the view opens across the immense interior of the hollow Moon.

The opening order is: first meeting → cell rescue → escape through the ruins and first vision → emergence and city reveal → saucer recovery and boarding → first meal and conversation.

Together, they recover her ship and escape immediate captivity.

## An unexpected partnership

Aboard the saucer, 7 tells the cat what she experienced. He helps her consider its meaning and offers possible places to investigate. He does not already know the answers.

She believes he is supposed to help her. He recognizes the importance of what is happening, likes her, and chooses to stay.

Their relationship begins with practical cooperation and gradually develops through jokes, philosophical conversations, danger and learning about each other’s races.

One planned comic moment occurs when they are hungry aboard the saucer. She asks whether he eats canned food. He says he prefers chicken—but only free-range. The exact dialogue remains open.

The cat normally stays aboard to watch the saucer and act as her contact. He can accompany her or come to help when circumstances call for it.

## The seven worlds

7 explores worlds associated with seven historical ages:

| Age | Civilization or race | World |
|---|---|---|
| I — First Garden Age | Elarim | Venus |
| II — Astronomical Age | Lunarians — name proposed | Earth’s hollow Moon |
| III — Great Machine Age | Machinists | Asteroid world |
| IV — Divided Kingdoms Age | Reptilians | Mars War Planet |
| V — Connected Mind Age | Biaviian | Biaveh |
| VI — Oceanic Age | Cetaceans | Europa |
| VII — Present Age | Feline race | Cattalus, with cardboard buildings |

These are historical associations, not a mandatory playing order. Worlds and their local adventures can be completed mostly in any order.

Each civilization has a temple and a rune preserving its records. The Elarim, Biaviian and Cetaceans are good guardians who request help against threats. Tunneler threatens the Moon, a Terminator-type antagonist threatens the asteroid, and Viper threatens Mars.

Ratwing and Wormmaster are established villains, but their proposed placements on Biaveh and Venus remain unapproved. Europa’s threat and Cattalus’s central conflict remain open.

7 never kills. She resolves threats through nonlethal means, with choices about intervention. The exact alternatives and their effects on rune access still need design.

A hover-bike race through Cattalus is a saved possibility, not yet an assigned mission.

## The history taking shape

Touching ruins allows 7 to experience the past. As she explores, the histories of the civilizations begin to connect.

She eventually discovers that she lived through the seven ages. Her own memories supply experiences and relationships missing from the civilizations’ separate records.

The recurring woman in historical visions is a proposed way to build toward this revelation. The living-archive origin, the reason for her forgotten memories and the precise rules behind the visions still require decisions.

The civilizations’ individual fates also remain unresolved. Some may have collapsed; others may have transformed or departed.

Her purpose becomes clear: she must preserve and pass on their shared history so it can survive without her.

## Tunneler’s opposition

Tunneler’s mining operation depends on his exploitation of the Scree. They are an oppressed people, including when they work or fight for him.

The accepted direction is that restoring history threatens his power. The proposed explanation is that recovered knowledge could undermine his energy monopoly and give the Scree the means to live independently. The specific technology, what he has concealed and how he controls them remain to be defined.

Helium-3 is a candidate resource for his operation, not yet a final choice.

7’s treatment of the Scree should reflect their situation. She can oppose those obstructing her without treating them as inherently evil. Some may help her, and they retain the agency to decide their own future.

Tunneler ultimately tries to prevent her from completing the transfer.

## The lunar chamber

With the runes assembled, 7 reaches the ancient lunar chamber connected to all seven temples.

The runes preserve the civilizations’ records. Her memories connect them into a fuller history. She understands both who she has been and what she must now accomplish.

The revelation is not the completion of her task. She must choose to share the whole truth, including failures and uncomfortable parts of her own history.

Before she can finish, Tunneler bursts in to stop her.

## The sacrifice

Tunneler launches a fatal attack intended for 7. The cat jumps into its path and takes the blow.

7 subdues Tunneler without killing him, then reaches her friend. He will not survive.

As he is dying, he whispers:

“Don’t worry, I have 8 more.”

He originally came because he was sent. His final act comes from the bond they have formed.

## Completing the task

After losing the cat, 7 completes the transfer.

The shared history becomes accessible through all seven temples. People can experience it, study it, copy it and teach it. Its survival no longer depends on one person or one irreplaceable location.

She gives the civilizations knowledge without choosing their future for them. Her journey with the cat becomes part of the history she preserves.

Her responsibility is fulfilled.

## The final scene

7 ascends to heaven.

There, she is reunited with the cat. The final scene shows them playing together.
