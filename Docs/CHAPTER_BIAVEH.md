## Story and continuity

The Biavian mothership hangs above Biaveh: an egg-shaped vessel **26 miles across its widest point**, large enough to contain entire districts, gardens and transport networks.

Ratwing has infiltrated its inner passages to seize the temple archive. He intends to control access to its accumulated knowledge and trade it for wealth and influence. **He has no alliance with Tunnler.**

To prevent the Biavians from coordinating against him, Ratwing has sabotaged three communication relays. The damage causes archived memories to leak into the living mental network, confusing residents and forcing districts into isolation.

7 helps reconnect the ship. Panther stays aboard their saucer, watching its systems and communicating privately with her. Ratwing’s appearance brings out a distinctly feline interest in him—but Panther’s attention becomes a practical advantage as he learns how Ratwing moves.

The chapter ends with Ratwing captured alive, the archive restored, and 7 receiving **the octahedron key and Biavian history**.

The mothership design, Ratwing’s role and independence, and the octahedron are established. Specific ship mechanisms, supporting characters and encounter details below are proposed implementation.

### Emotional thread

Among a people who share thoughts, 7 discovers that she can choose what to share. Panther offers companionship without demanding access to her memories. After the temple vision, she chooses to tell him because she trusts him.

### Progression

**Mars and Europa complete → depart for Biaveh → discover asteroid en route → Biaveh and asteroid in either order → Cattalus → final Moon.**

#### Setting correction

The Biavian mothership is stationed among the rings of **Veyra**, a planet outside our solar system. Veyra is not the Biavian homeworld.

The combined Mars and Europa records identify an old Biavian contact route. Following it leads to the mothership’s station near Veyra, rather than to Biaveh itself.

The asteroid encounter still happens along this journey. Players can investigate it first or continue to the mothership.

#### Mothership exterior

- An immense egg-shaped vessel, **26 miles across its widest point**.
- **Three protective orbs revolve around it**, clearly separate from ordinary ship traffic.
- Its entrance is a **mechanical iris**, with overlapping segments that rotate open like a camera aperture.
- The iris opens only wide enough to admit the approaching ship, then closes behind it.

The protective orbs defend the exterior. Ratwing is already inside, where they cannot intervene without endangering the inhabitants.

### Nonlethal encounter

The objective is to **contain Ratwing**, not defeat him by lethal damage.

**Stage one — restrict his flight**

Ratwing makes clearly telegraphed passes at 7 and the relay controls. She dodges behind structural cover and activates partitions after he passes.

Each successful activation reduces the chamber’s open flight space.

**Stage two — protect the controls**

He lands on platforms to break the mechanisms. 7 uses the bike to reach alternate control stations while Panther calls out his movements.

**Stage three — close the last exit**

Ratwing appears to retreat, but Panther recognizes that he is circling toward the final service opening.

The player waits for the correct moment, then closes the outer barrier after Ratwing enters the empty maintenance compartment. A second barrier seals behind him, leaving him contained in a spacious enclosure.

The barriers must never close on his body or wings. Mistimed attempts reset the opportunity.
