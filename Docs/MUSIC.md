# Music — 7: The Observer (Chapter 1)

Made by Dan in **Suno** (Instrumental on). Chapter 1 is short, so it uses just **three tracks**. Save each as MP3 in
`Unity/Assets/Audio/Music/` with the file name given; Code's music system picks them up by name (missing = silence).
Mood for the whole game: Kyle's art — misty, glowing, gentle, mysterious, a little melancholy; never bombastic.

| File | Title | Where it plays | Style prompt (paste into Suno) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `main_theme.mp3` | The Observer | Main menu; swells at **the city reveal**; returns for the tractor beam / liftoff | ethereal ambient orchestral, soft wordless female vocal, gentle piano, warm string pads, glassy bells, slow build to a wide hopeful swell, mysterious and tender, cinematic |
| `moon_prison.mp3` | Beneath the Mine | Everything inside the prison and ruins: cell, corridors, sneaking past the Scree, ancient passage | dark ambient, low drones, distant industrial machinery rhythm, metallic echoes, sparse lonely piano notes, faint low choir, cold cavern reverb, slow and uneasy, minimal, loopable |
| `aboard_saucer.mp3` | Free-Range | Aboard the saucer (interior, galley, the cat staying, free exploration after the chapter) | cozy retro-futurist lounge, soft electric piano, brushed drums, warm bass, light vibraphone, gently playful and warm, late-night calm, loopable |

The first vision has **no music** — it fades out and the vision's own shimmer sound carries the moment.
