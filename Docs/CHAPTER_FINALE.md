# THE MOON FINALE — "Somewhere to come back to" (chapter script)

> **Status (2026-11-22):** finale script from Dan. The last chapter: after Cattalus (CAT_12's Moon route). No new 3D models.
> To be built as **Round 19**. Planning-chat notes and decisions are at the end ("Decisions and reconciliations"); Dan's script
> below is unchanged except that the villain's name is spelled **Tunneler** in the game (the script writes "Tunnler"; same character,
> voice name `Tunneler`).

## Finale story

7 and Panther return to the hollow Moon with all seven pieces.

Beneath the city, they discover a chamber built to reunite the civilizations’ surviving records. Its archive recognizes 7 because she witnessed those civilizations herself. The visions were fragments of her own memories.

The records contain knowledge. Her memories preserve the people behind it: their kindness, disagreements, ordinary pleasures and acts of care.

Her task is to bring those histories together and pass them on.

Tunnler has kept the chamber inaccessible while exploiting the Scree and stripping the surrounding lunar structures for his mining operation. Opening the archive restores a public network he has monopolized. The Scree gain access to their own history, independent communication and control of the infrastructure they maintain.

He comes to stop the release.

Panther intercepts an attack meant for 7. She contains Tunnler without killing him, but cannot save Panther.

She completes the transmission. The histories reach the worlds they visited. The Scree take control of the lunar operation.

With her task accomplished, 7 ascends. The final reunion returns to the meadow with Panther—in full color.

### Decisions used in this draft

- 7 can be hurt but does not age or die as ordinary people do. The exact limits remain unexplained.
- Panther acts because he sees an immediate threat. Neither he nor 7 treats her survival as guaranteed protection.
- Tunnler loses control through a combination of restored access, Scree action and his physical capture.
- The archive does not automatically repair every society or erase suffering.
- No physical deity, angel, new guardian or new enemy model appears.

## Pacing and implementation

- Give the return journey some warmth before the revelation.
- Keep the rune placement sequence short.
- Let the player choose to share the history.
- Establish the containment barrier before it is needed.
- Make Panther’s interception a story event, not a failed player skill check.
- Tunnler’s capture ends the combat permanently.
- Remove objective prompts and celebratory UI during Panther’s death.
- Allow silence before asking the player to resume the archive.
- Show the Scree taking practical control of their own systems.
- Confirm that the histories have reached others before 7 ascends.
- Save full color for the heavenly reunion.
- End on companionship, not an explanation.
