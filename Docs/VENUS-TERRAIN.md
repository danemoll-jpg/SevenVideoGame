# Venus: natural ground, not an elevated path (Dan, 2026-11-18) — add to QF10 as item 6f

Dan: the world seems to be **on some sort of elevated path instead of just land**. Not a huge open world — but it must
look **natural**: Venus is the First Garden Age, a completely **organic** world.

- **Real ground.** Replace the raised road / walkway blocks with continuous sculpted terrain (Unity Terrain or a
  sculpted ground mesh): rolling grassy slopes, hollows, low rises, rock outcrops — land that goes all the way out to
  the fog, not platforms over a void. Nowhere should the player see the underside or edge of the world.
- **The route is a path worn into the land**, following the contours (gentle curves around hills, dipping and rising),
  bordered by grass and plants rather than kerbs or walls. Wide and smooth enough for the bike; clearly readable as
  the way to go (worn ground, the temple visible ahead, lamps / stones along it).
- **Organic architecture grows out of the ground:** terraces read as natural shelves of the hillside planted with
  gardens; channels are streams and grown waterways in the land; the temple grows from a hill; the landing platform
  sits on a natural rise.
- **Boundaries are natural** — steep slopes, dense vegetation, rock, water, fog — not invisible walls or drops.
- **The collapse (VEN_B03) becomes a break in the land:** a stream has undercut the path and the ground has fallen into a
  gully / sinkhole; the fallen slab (or a toppled grown column / great root) beside it is what 7 lifts across with
  telekinesis. Same flow as the spec.
- Keep the scope: a natural valley corridor, not open world. Keep everything Round 11 placed (scenes, triggers,
  worker spots, observation area, the basin, the maintenance path) — move them onto the new ground; the grass (item 3)
  goes on the new terrain.

  `kyle_7_riding_city`, `kyle_7_riding_fog`.
