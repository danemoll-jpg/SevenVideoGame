> **Status (2026-11-19):** Europa chapter spec from Dan. Europa’s rune is an **icosahedron** (Dan). Tunneler is the threat in this chapter.

**EUROPA — BENEATH THE ICE**

*Panther’s dialogue is telepathic. His voice can carry breaths, chuckles, and hesitation without mouth animation. Use restrained physical performances: changes in posture, hands becoming still, head turns, and pauses.*

*This chapter works whether visited before or after Mars.*

---

**1. ARRIVAL — A WORLD BENEATH THE SURFACE**

**EXTREME WIDE — EUROPA**

The saucer crosses an immense pale ice field. Fractures wind beneath it like branching veins.

**INT. SAUCER — PILOT’S CHAIR**

7 sits with Panther in her lap. Ahead, the Elarim’s coordinates mark a location beneath the surface.

**7:** There’s an ocean under all of this.

**PANTHER:** Apparently.

**7:** You don’t sound curious.

**PANTHER:** I’m curious from here.

She laughs softly and strokes his back.

**INSERT — NAVIGATION DISPLAY**

A passage through the ice appears near the temple coordinates.

**7:** There. A way down.

Panther looks up at her.

**PANTHER:** You said that rather enthusiastically.

**7 — smiling in her voice:** Mm-hmm.

---

**2. THE BIKE — PREPARING TO DIVE**

**WIDE — LANDING AREA**

The saucer hovers above a sheltered shelf beside an opening in the ice.

Its underside beam lowers the hover bike into position. 7 descends through the beam wearing her bubble helmet.

Panther watches from within the ship.

**INSERT — HELMET**

7 checks the seal. A soft tone confirms it.

**INSERT — RECESSED HANDHOLDS**

She settles her hands into the bike’s fairing.

**PANTHER:** That is the same bike.

**7:** Yes.

**PANTHER:** And you’re taking it underwater.

**7:** It works underwater.

A beat.

**PANTHER:** I’m pleased one of us knew that.

**7 — a small laugh:** Keep an eye on the ship.

**PANTHER:** Happily.

**GAMEPLAY**

The player guides the bike down a gentle ice passage into the water. Teach underwater steering, ascent, descent, and braking in a safe space.

---

**3. FIRST DESCENT — WONDER**

**WIDE — BELOW THE ICE**

The bike enters an enormous underwater chamber.

Light filters through the ceiling. Long curtains of bubbles rise through fractures. Ancient structures descend into the depths, their windows and pathways marked by small white lights.

Far below, vast shapes move slowly through the water.

Allow the player to ride without interruption.

**7 — softly:** Oh…

**PANTHER:** What is it?

**7:** They’re beneath me.

**PANTHER:** Who?

A Cetacean passes beneath the bike. Its scale becomes apparent as it continues beyond the frame.

**7 — an astonished breath:** I think one of the people we came to meet.

A low, complex call travels through the water.

7 slows.

**7:** Did you hear that?

**PANTHER:** Through your connection. A little.

**7:** I wish you could see this.

**PANTHER — gently:** Tell me.

Let the next stretch remain quiet.

---

**4. THE CETACEAN SETTLEMENT**

**GAMEPLAY — APPROACH**

A Cetacean guide accompanies the bike toward inhabited structures built around open swimming passages.

Small vehicles move between platforms. Broad chambers accommodate the Cetaceans themselves.

**PROPOSED COMMUNICATION CONVENTION**

Cetacean speech is presented as a comprehensible voice accompanied by their calls. Its underlying explanation can be decided later; do not add an exposition scene here.

**WIDE — MEETING SPACE**

7 stops at a receiving platform, still wearing her helmet. The guide turns in the water beside her.

**KEEPER:** You followed the old approach.

**7:** The Elarim gave us the location.

**KEEPER:** Then someone still remembers the route.

**7:** We’re looking for your temple.

The keeper’s attention shifts to the geometric records she carries.

**KEEPER:** And you have brought other histories with you.

**7:** I’m trying to understand them.

A distant mechanical pulse interrupts the water.

The keeper becomes still.

**7:** What was that?

**KEEPER:** Something that does not belong here.

---

**5. PLAY BEFORE THE DANGER — THE RACE ROUTE**

Before the main mission advances, allow exploration of the settlement and an optional underwater race.

The course passes through carved arches, beneath ice shelves, and around great pillars. It teaches the movement skills needed later in the escape.

No important historical reward requires winning here. Cattalus remains the required championship race.

**AT REGISTRATION**

**7:** They have races.

**PANTHER:** Of course that’s what you noticed.

**7:** It would help me learn the bike down here.

**PANTHER:** A thoroughly educational decision.

**7 — laughing:** Exactly.

**DURING A CLEAN OVERTAKE**

**7:** Ha!

**PANTHER:** Was that educational?

**7:** Very.

**AFTER A LOSS**

**7 — breathless chuckle:** I took that turn too wide.

**PANTHER:** The pillar seemed to agree.

**AFTER A WIN**

**PANTHER:** Shall I add professional racing to our itinerary?

**7:** Just visiting the temple will do.

A distant extraction pulse sounds again.

The mood settles.

---

**6. WHAT THE MACHINERY IS DOING**

**OVER 7’S SHOULDER — DAMAGED PASSAGE**

The keeper shows her a migration route interrupted by drilling infrastructure.

The damage is visible: shattered supports, suspended debris, a sealed passage, creatures turning away from the mechanical noise.

**KEEPER:** Our young travel through here. Our calls guide them.

Another pulse overwhelms a distant Cetacean call.

**KEEPER:** Now they cannot hear us.

**7:** Who built it?

**KEEPER:** He calls himself Tunneler.

7’s hand tightens on the bike’s fairing.

**PANTHER — quietly:** Oh. Him.

**7:** We know the name.

**KEEPER:** He promised to take only what we would not miss.

**7:** Where is he?

The keeper turns toward a distant industrial structure embedded beneath the ice.

**KEEPER:** Below the old shelf. He has brought workers who cannot leave.

**7:** The Scree?

**KEEPER:** Yes.

A pause.

**7 — firmly:** Show me the way in.

**OBJECTIVE:** Investigate Tunneler’s underwater base.

---

**7. APPROACH — QUIET OBSERVATION**

**GAMEPLAY**

7 follows the bike route toward the base. The playful course geometry gives way to pipes, intake channels, and industrial tunnels.

She observes the operation before entering.

Show Scree workers laboring near equipment. Their movements establish fatigue and coercion.

**7:** They’re working down here too.

**PANTHER:** Can you reach them?

**7:** There’s a service entrance.

**PANTHER:** Find a way out as well.

**7:** I will.

She parks the bike in a sheltered access bay and enters on foot through a pressure chamber.

Retain the bubble helmet where environmental conditions require it.

---

**8. FIRST CONTACT WITH TUNNELER**

**LOW-ANGLE SHOT — INDUSTRIAL DISPLAY**

As 7 enters a restricted control area, Tunneler appears on a screen. Machinery lights his shape from below.

**TUNNELER:** You.

**MEDIUM — 7**

She stops, then looks directly at the display.

**7:** You remember me.

**TUNNELER:** You were supposed to remain where you were put.

**7:** I didn’t like the accommodation.

**PANTHER — privately, dryly:** Neither did I.

**TUNNELER:** Leave my operation alone.

**7:** Your machinery is closing their routes. Your workers can’t leave.

**TUNNELER:** My workers have obligations.

**7:** Then you can explain them when the doors are open.

Tunneler leans closer to the camera.

**TUNNELER:** You have no idea what it takes to keep all of this running.

**7:** I’m starting to.

The screen goes dark. Security barriers close.

**GAMEPLAY**

The player must reach the worker holding area and restore an evacuation route.

Do not require killing guards or workers.

---

**9. FREEING THE SCREE**

**WIDE — WORKER AREA**

7 opens a secured passage. A Scree worker hesitates at the threshold.

**SCREE WORKER:** Where are we being taken?

7 steps aside, leaving the doorway clear.

**7 — gently:** Out.

The worker looks toward the others.

**SCREE WORKER:** All of us?

**7:** All of you.

Keep this moment simple.

**GAMEPLAY**

Open transport access, restore power to an evacuation lift, and guide workers away from the extraction machinery.

Some workers can help operate doors or identify safe routes. Give them agency rather than making them passive followers.

A worker shows 7 a schematic.

**SCREE WORKER:** These connections feed the drills. Break them, and he cannot restart them.

**7:** What happens to the base?

The worker indicates an isolated machinery section and the evacuation passages.

**SCREE WORKER:** This section will not survive.

7 studies the route.

**7:** Then nobody stays in it.

---

**10. PREPARING THE DESTRUCTION**

**PROPOSED GAMEPLAY**

7 places fictional demolition devices at designated machinery connections. Keep the interaction abstract.

Separate the evacuation objective from the detonation objective. Detonation remains unavailable until occupied areas and nearby creature routes are clear.

Panther tracks the departing transports through the saucer’s instruments.

**PANTHER:** First transport is away.

**7:** And the others?

**PANTHER:** Still boarding.

7 reaches the final objective.

**INSERT — HER HAND ON THE LAST DEVICE**

**7:** Ready here.

**PANTHER:** Then wait.

She looks toward the access tunnel. An alarm begins.

Hold the tension without starting a timer that encourages abandoning workers.

**PANTHER:** Last transport moving.

A pause.

**PANTHER — relieved breath:** They’re clear.

---

**11. TUNNELER ESCAPES**

**WIDE — SEPARATED DOCKING CHAMBERS**

On her way back to the bike, 7 sees Tunneler entering his escape craft across a sealed observation window.

He sees her.

**TUNNELER:** You think this changes anything?

**7:** They’re leaving. That changes something.

His craft powers up.

**TUNNELER:** You cannot follow me everywhere.

**7:** I wasn’t following you.

He looks toward the failing control displays, then back at her.

The craft departs through its own passage.

**PANTHER:** Let him go. Your exit is the other way.

7 turns toward her bike.

This is not a failed boss fight. Her objective is freeing the workers and stopping the operation, and she has achieved it.

---

**12. THE ESCAPE — FULL SPEED**

**INSERT — 7 MOUNTING THE BIKE**

Her hands settle into the recessed grips.

**7:** Panther?

**PANTHER:** I’m here.

**7:** Everyone clear?

**PANTHER:** Everyone we brought out. The keeper has cleared the water beyond the machinery section.

She triggers the demolition.

**EXTREME WIDE — THE BASE**

White flashes erupt inside the extraction structure. Power fails in sections.

**GAMEPLAY — EXIT TUNNEL**

7 accelerates.

A chain of ruptures follows her through the industrial passage: expanding bubbles, debris, and structural failures. The destruction advances behind her in a controlled cinematic sequence.

Give the player readable steering choices and sufficient warning.

**PANTHER — focused:** Left at the junction.

**7:** I see it.

She banks around a broken support.

**PANTHER:** The next opening—

**7:** I see it!

A collapsing section narrows the route.

**LOW TRACKING SHOT — BIKE FORKS**

The bike clears the obstruction.

Return immediately to the riding camera.

The open ocean appears ahead.

**PANTHER:** Keep going.

7 bursts out of the tunnel.

**WIDE — OPEN WATER**

The entrance collapses behind her. A pressure wave catches the bike and throws it off line.

She struggles briefly, then regains control.

Do not cut away before the player sees her recover.

---

**13. AFTER THE NOISE**

7 brings the bike to a stop in clear water.

Her breathing is audible inside the helmet.

**7 — catching her breath:** Hah… okay.

A beat.

**PANTHER:** 7?

**7:** I’m here.

**PANTHER:** Stay there a moment.

His voice is quieter now.

She loosens her grip.

**7:** I’m all right.

A pause.

**PANTHER — returning to his usual dryness:** And the bike?

She gives a breathless laugh.

**7:** Also all right.

**PANTHER:** Good. I was running out of things to worry about.

**CLOSE-UP — 7’S EYES THROUGH THE HELMET**

Her gaze softens. No complex facial performance is required.

Before she answers, a Cetacean call travels through the water.

Another answers.

**WIDE — THE OCEAN**

With the machinery silent, the calls carry across the open space.

**7 — softly:** Listen.

Hold the moment.

---

**14. THE TEMPLE — WHAT SURVIVES**

**WIDE — CETACEAN TEMPLE**

The keeper leads 7 through the reopened route.

The temple should feel built for its inhabitants: broad passages, enormous spaces, and architecture understood through movement and sound.

**KEEPER:** We can hear the young again.

**7:** The workers are safe?

**KEEPER:** They are being welcomed.

7 lets out a small relieved breath.

**KEEPER:** Come. You asked about our history.

The keeper brings her to Europa’s rune.

**DESIGN NOTE (Dan, 2026-11-19):** Europa’s rune is an **icosahedron**. (Venus = cube; Moon = dodecahedron.)

---

**15. EUROPA’S VISION — PROPOSED**

**VISION VIDEO (Dan, 2026-11-19):** `Art/Video/vision_03_europa.mp4` (12 s, 1920×1044 like the Venus vision; still fallback `Resources/Europa/vision_03_still.jpg`). It shows a large Cetacean and a small one swimming together through a misty, glowing passage of great arches. Its own audio is nearly silent, so the young-and-old call (`europa_call_learning`) carries the scene: the small one’s wobbly attempt, the large one’s clear answer, the small one again. Play it the same way as the Venus vision (music suspended, vision shimmer in and out).

This vision should differ from Venus’s intimate planting memory.

**Proposed content:** A small group of Cetaceans travels through an intact passage, exchanging calls. A younger one struggles to reproduce a short pattern. An older one repeats it patiently. The younger one tries again.

7 experiences an unexpected familiarity with the answering pattern.

No catastrophe, death, or explanation of immortality is shown.

The scene ends on the shared call.

**BACK IN THE TEMPLE**

7 remains still.

**KEEPER:** What did you hear?

**7:** Someone learning.

**KEEPER:** That is how much of our history travelled. One voice teaching another.

She looks toward the passage.

**7:** I knew what came next.

**KEEPER:** In the record?

**7:** Before they answered.

The keeper allows a silence.

**KEEPER:** Then you have brought a question we cannot answer for you.

The keeper shares Europa’s record using the same collection rules adopted for the other worlds.

**INSERT — 7 secures the small icosahedron beside the cube and the dodecahedron.**

**KEEPER:** Carry it somewhere it can be heard.

This connects Europa to passing on history without resolving the larger mystery.

---

**16. RETURN TO THE SAUCER — A QUIET REUNION**

**WIDE — SAUCER ABOVE THE ICE**

The bike returns to the boarding position and is recovered through the underside beam.

7 boards through the beam. No ramp.

**INT. SAUCER**

She removes her bubble helmet.

Panther is waiting near the boarding area, rather than in his usual comfortable spot.

She notices.

**7 — gently teasing:** Were you waiting for the bike?

**PANTHER:** Someone had to inspect the damage.

She kneels beside him.

**7:** No damage.

He presses his head against her hand.

A quiet pause.

**PANTHER:** Good.

Let her remain with him for a moment before either moves.

---

**17. DEBRIEF — AN ORDINARY SOUND**

Later, 7 sits in the pilot’s chair with Panther in her lap.

Europa’s record rests with the others.

**7:** They teach their history through sound.

Panther gives a thoughtful hum.

**PANTHER:** Sensible. Difficult to hold a book down there.

She chuckles.

**7:** I heard a call in the vision. I knew the answer before it came.

Her hand slows against his fur.

**7:** How could I know that?

**PANTHER — gently:** I don’t know.

No attempt to turn that answer into a joke.

He begins to purr.

She looks down at him, listening.

**7 — quietly:** That one I know.

Her hand resumes its movement.

Hold the two-shot before returning to navigation.

**END OF EUROPA CHAPTER**

- Tunneler survives and remembers this confrontation.
- Europa’s record is awarded once.
