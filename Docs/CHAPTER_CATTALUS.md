## Chapter story

After completing the Biavian mothership and asteroid chapters, 7 and Panther follow the combined records to Cattalus.

Panther brings her home.

The city is playful and unmistakably feline: elaborate cardboard houses, cat-tree apartments, scratching-post columns, dangling toys and carefully occupied patches of sunlight. For once, Panther accompanies 7 throughout their visit.

She learns that he volunteered for the assignment to rescue her. He was sent, as he told her—but he asked to go. Staying afterward was his own choice.

On the Cat-a-tron, they recognize the final rune in a racing trophy: **a smooth sphere**. Its custodians have preserved it for generations while forgetting its original purpose.

7 enters the hover-bike race. Winning earns the trophy directly. Losing earns a magnificent cardboard retreat, which the champion willingly exchanges for the trophy.

Touching the sphere gives 7 the last memory: a traveler entrusting it to a cat beneath a leaning tree. Panther recognizes the place and takes her there.

In the meadow, he reveals his greatest form. They run and play, then rest together. This becomes the visual foundation for their eventual reunion in heaven.

Back aboard the saucer, the complete set of records resolves a route to a chamber beneath the lunar city. They leave to finish their journey.

### Essential continuity

- Cattalus is the last destination before the Moon finale.
- The final rune is a **sphere**.
- Winning and losing the completed race both lead to obtaining it.
- Panther accompanies 7 on Cattalus.
- The saucer uses an underside beam, never a ramp.
- Cattalus and its vision remain grayscale.
- Generate the meadow cinematic in full color, then derive its grayscale version.
- Reuse the original color footage for the heavenly reunion.
- The complete explanation of 7’s identity and purpose remains for the Moon.

## How this chapter connects the visions

The visions have shown lived moments: people planting, building, teaching, repairing and trusting one another.

The Cattalus memory completes that pattern with the willing transfer of something precious.

It does not explain every mystery. It provides:

- The final historical contribution.
- A visual connection to the Moon’s first rune.
- Recognition of a place Panther knows.
- A transition from an inherited memory to a happy experience 7 shares with him herself.

The assembled records provide the actual lunar route.

The lunar chamber supplies the complete revelation: the records preserve the civilizations’ knowledge, while 7’s lived memories complete their history. Her task is to bring them together and pass them on.

- Use ears, tails, posture, hand placement and camera framing to support emotion.
- Keep laughs, breaths and hums natural and selective.
- Generate clean video, then add dialogue, laughter, breathing, purring and music separately.
- Approve one full-color reference of 7 beside giant Panther before generating the field clips.
- Preserve the same character designs, scale, leaning tree, hill line and light direction across segments.
- Save the full-color master; apply grayscale only to the Cattalus presentation.

### Chapter tone

The world should feel inhabited and affectionate, not like a collection of disconnected cat jokes.

Give the player time to explore, laugh, race and sit with the characters. The emotional impact of the finale depends on this chapter feeling like a good day they genuinely enjoyed together.

### The race (CAT_05 – CAT_08)
- **Two racers:** 7 on her bike and Duchess on her hover racer (open hovercraft class).
- **One lap of the Grand Circuit, about 2½ minutes**, through the six sections in the script. Gates are Cattalus things (hanging
  ornament arches, banner posts), with a soft glow on the next one. The toy boulevard's swinging obstacles swing on a fixed,
  readable rhythm.
- **Duchess's AI:** rides a good racing line at a pace a clean rider can beat and a sloppy one can't. Only a mild catch-up,
  invisible; no cheating speeds.
- **Crashes:** back on the course at the last gate with a 3-second penalty (shown as a small time note, bottom of the screen).
  "Restart race" in the pause menu; leaving the course for too long offers a restart.
- **Result:** saved (`CattalusState.RaceResult` = Won / RunnerUp), shown in the Log. Both branches join at CAT_09; the sphere is
  awarded once.
- **Race camera:** `ChapterCamera.RaceMode` (Europa) for steady, readable framing.
- **Practice lap:** optional, no opponent, the same gates; Panther's lines from CAT_06 play as tips on the first practice only.
- Panther's lines are tied to course positions (the market bend, the heights) and to whether she's leading or behind.
