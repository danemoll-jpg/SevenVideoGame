WHAT WAS WRONG (Round 3 rig)
- Joints were fixed fractions of the shoulder height, not fitted: the upper legs were vertical sticks from 0.66 H down to
  a "knee" at 0.40 H and an ankle at 0.13 H for all four legs. The hind leg had no real stifle / hock (hock sat 3 cm off the
  floor), the elbow sat 2 cm too low, so the legs bent in the wrong places.
- The neck bone ran almost straight up into the back of the skull and the head bone started above the ears.
- Weights were inverse-distance^4 to the 3 nearest bones with hard cut-offs (leg bones ignored anything above 0.72 H, across
  x = +-4 mm, or past the body middle; head/neck stopped at a fixed y; tail stopped at a fixed y). The cut-offs made seams:
  dark tears at the chest / shoulder in every walk frame, the belly and chest dragged by the front legs, a crumpled thigh
  in sit/wash, and collapsed triangles in sleep. No smoothing over the mesh.

WHAT CHANGED
- Joint fitting from the mesh: each leg is traced up from the paw in 4 mm slabs (centre of the slab section, mean-shift
  with a 3 mm/step drift limit so it keeps the leg's line where it opens into the body). Elbow = rearmost point of the
  front leg under the chest, wrist at 0.12 H; stifle = foremost point of the thigh, hock = rearmost point of the lower hind
  leg (now 6 cm high), hip about above the hock, shoulder just ahead of the elbow; shoulders/hips mirrored left-right.
  Spine points = 58% up the torso cross-section (legs and the tail excluded); neck from the front of the chest to an atlas
  joint just behind/below the skull centre (mean-shift on the head); tail re-centred on its cross-section every step.
- Weights: Blender bone heat (automatic weights, solved at 10x scale because heat is unreliable at 0.25 m), then soft
  guards (a leg never moves the other side or the other end of the body; upper legs fade out over the back; tail fades out
  in front of the hips), 4 Laplacian smoothing passes over the mesh edges, weights < 1% dropped, max 4 influences,
  normalized. 0 unweighted vertices. Whiskers stay 100% on "head".
- Clips: no pose angles changed; idle/walk/sit/wash/jump/sleep keep their names, frame counts and grounding. The wash paw
  now reaches the face instead of sinking into the chest (it follows from the corrected elbow / shoulder).

UNCHANGED (Unity contract)
- 24 bones, same names (root, pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head, tail1-6, FL/FR/HL/HR _upper/_lower/_foot); root does not
  deform. Head still forward (-Y in Blender -> +Z after the builder's head/pelvis facing fix). 0.25 m shoulder, origin
  between the feet on the floor, 18000 tris + 10 whisker tris, same file and texture paths, same 6 takes.
  Final size x0.156 y0.411 z0.398 m. Clip grounding (lowest body point): idle +-2 mm, walk/sit/wash/sleep 0, jump 0 on the
  ground (0.119 m at the top of the jump).
