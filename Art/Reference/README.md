# Reference art — 7: The Observer

Concept art by Kyle G. Anderson unless noted. Original file names in brackets.
Note: some images carry a red "7" logo; the game itself stays strictly monochrome.

**How to use Kyle's character art:** use it for mood, lighting, fog/glow, hair, collar, forehead mark and expression.
Some of the older pieces show a high-cut bodysuit or posed, body-focused framing; those predate the confirmed rule that
7 is presented non-sexually (full-coverage outfit, neutral poses, no camera emphasis on her body). Follow the approved
model (`7_ref_regular_*.png`) for her outfit and framing, not those pieces.
(This applies to building the game. The in-game art gallery shows Kyle's pieces as drawn — Dan, 2026-11-17.)

Open design notes from these pieces (decide with Dan/Kyle, don't change the model on your own):
- **Hair ties:** Kyle's art shows **glowing round clips**; the model has polished metal cuffs. Dan (2026-11-17): the
  hairband "probably can be changed" — switch to glowing clips is queued in TODO Next Steps, pending Dan's go.
- **Forehead mark — DECIDED (Kyle via Dan, 2026-11-17): a plain glowing dot, her "energy point"** (keep the model's dot).
  The yin-yang pieces are older art. Kyle's gameplay proposal: its brightness shows her remaining energy for the shield and telekinesis.
- **"7" emblem:** a circled "7" appears on her back (`kyle_7_backpack_saucer` on a backpack, `kyle_7_peak_sun` on the suit);
  the current model has no emblem.
- **Saucer exterior:** Kyle's saucer is a wide, flat disc with a glowing rim and a dark glossy hull
  (`kyle_7_backpack_saucer`, `kyle_7_on_hull`); Round 3 built only the interior.
